# PURPOSE

This document defines the complete Vendor System of the TRUEDIAL Platform.

The Vendor System is the primary revenue-generating module of the platform.

Everything should help vendors grow their business.

The platform succeeds only if vendors succeed.

---

# WHO IS A VENDOR?

A Vendor is any registered business or professional offering products or services through the platform.

Examples

• Interior Designer

• Architect

• Restaurant

• Gym

• Doctor

• Salon

• Real Estate Agency

• Photographer

• Electrician

• Lawyer

• Tutor

• Retail Store

• Manufacturer

Every vendor follows the same architecture.

# FINAL RULE

The Vendor Dashboard is not an admin panel.

It is a Business Growth Operating System.

Every feature must answer one question:

**"Does this help the vendor grow their business?"**

If the answer is no, it should not be part of the Vendor System.
